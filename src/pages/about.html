Somebody is reading your site’s source code. It isn’t a customer.

Remote-first · US-based · Founded 2026

Unfortunately, online stores are hit with thousands of predatory accessibility lawsuits every year.1

The reason is simple: those stores cannot be used by shoppers who are blind or who have trouble using a mouse. More than 1 in 4 American adults live with a disability.2 Many navigate the web with a screen reader, or with a keyboard instead of a mouse, and a store that ignores them is a store they cannot buy from.

Lawyers find those stores with free automated tests, the same ones anyone can run: Lighthouse, WAVE, axe. They scan thousands of sites at a time. A poor score does not prove a store broke the law, but it makes a store cheap to document, and that is enough to earn a demand letter. I know, because a client of my marketing agency, Crier Communications, got one. That is what led me to start Accessrank.

Many stores attempt to fix accessibility issues with a plugin. These are overlays: a script that sits on top of your existing code and tries to patch problems when a site is visited, leaving the source code untouched. Vendors have claimed for years that this makes a site compliant. It does not. In April 2025 the FTC ordered the best known of them, accessiBe, to pay $1 million over product claims the Commission found false, misleading or unsubstantiated.3 We fix the code, and then keep it fixed.

Beyond lowering legal exposure, an accessible store reaches more customers. More than 70 million American adults have a disability.2 They are potential customers most stores turn away at the door without ever knowing it.

Accessibility work also helps your SEO, because screen readers and search engines read the same structure: image descriptions, heading order, link text and more.4

We decided to go a little deeper and write the code that AI search and shopping assistants need to find your store and your products: product facts written in a form machines can read, descriptive page titles and summaries, buttons and links an agent can follow, labelled form fields, and crawler access for AI search. We report that as an AI readiness score alongside your accessibility and SEO scores, never folded into them. That has a little something to do with why our name has both access and rank in it.

What will Accessrank not claim?

  • We will not claim instant or permanent compliance. Conformance is a snapshot of the pages we assessed, at the time we assessed them, and it needs monitoring to stay true.
  • We will not guarantee that remediation prevents a demand letter or lawsuit. It reduces risk; it cannot eliminate it.
  • We are not a law firm, and nothing we publish is legal advice; see our Disclosure.
  • We will not tell you a score alone proves conformance. Automated testing catches a real but limited share of barriers; see how we actually calculate a score on our methodology page.

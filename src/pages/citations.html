Every statistic on this site is drawn from a named third-party source. This page lists them in one place. Figures describe the market generally, not a specific result you should expect — see our Disclosure for details.

  • More than 1 in 4 American adults live with a disability.

    Source: CDC, Disability and Health Data System (DHDS), 2022 BRFSS data.

  • 77% of ADA lawsuits target ecommerce sites.

    Source: UsableNet 2024 Year-End Report.

  • 4,605 ADA website lawsuits were filed in 2024 alone, with a 37% surge in early 2025.

    Source: UsableNet ADA Lawsuit Tracker.

  • 95.9% of websites fail WCAG 2.2.

    Source: WebAIM Million 2025.

  • Higher-accessibility-score sites average 23% more organic traffic.

    Source: Semrush × AccessibilityChecker.org, 2023 (847 sites). A larger 2025 Semrush study of 10,000 sites found higher-accessibility-score sites averaged 23% more organic traffic and 27% more ranking keywords. Correlational, not causal — individual results vary.

  • The average ADA lawsuit costs $30K+ in combined settlement and legal fees.

    Source: Accessible.org lawsuit analysis (2024).

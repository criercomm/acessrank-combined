If a letter arrives

Every plan comes with an Accessibility Compliance Record: a dated baseline, a fix-by-fix log, a VPAT-format conformance report, a published accessibility statement and a signed certificate after every scan. It answers the three questions that decide whether a demand letter goes away or becomes a lawsuit: what you fixed, when, and whether you are still at it. If a letter arrives while you are on a plan, we prepare a barrier-by-barrier response and a checklist for your counsel within two business days.

About the numbers. The automated score is a conformance-criterion pass rate: weighted criteria met ÷ weighted criteria evaluated × 100, with Level A weighted 3, Level AA 2 and best-practice checks 1 (see {{ site_host }}/methodology). It covers only the criteria automated testing could evaluate on the {{ site.pagesLabel }} scanned, which is why it is a best case: the full audit evaluates all {{ access.wcagTotal }} criteria and usually finds more.
